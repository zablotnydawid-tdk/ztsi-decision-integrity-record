# DIR-001 Attestation Boundary

DIR-001 records a decision-integrity execution state.

It must not be represented as:

- certification by Meta
- endorsement by Meta
- accreditation
- regulatory certification
- government approval
- court validation
- legal notarization
- qualified electronic signature
- universal proof that every underlying factual proposition is true

## Cryptographic Meaning

The SHA-256 fingerprint establishes identity and integrity of the exact 541-byte canonical representation when independently recomputed.

It does not transform the record into legal authority.
